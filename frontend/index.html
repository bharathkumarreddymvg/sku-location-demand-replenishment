Stockout-Aware Demand Forecasting & Constrained Replenishment

This system simulates 2,000 SKUs across 100 stores over 365 days (2025-01-16 to 2026-01-14). At the temporal cutoff date 2025-12-31, it generates 14-day forecasts for forecast_period = 2026-01 (2026-01-01 through 2026-01-14). By filtering out stockout-censored days (stockout_censored_flag == True), our model cuts holdout WAPE from 19.65% (Simple Naive Baseline) down to 12.35% (a 37.15% error reduction), and recommends replenishment orders with full decision_trace auditability across MOQ, pack size, and both SKU-shelf and store-level capacity limits.
